4. Strategic Content Recommendations

Four clear directives based on the 33-post Meta analytics audit.

Action Plan
01. Solve the "Save" Deficit

Build Keepable Checklists

Saves sit at only 38 lifetime. People like the posts, but do not yet see them as a reference tool. We will incorporate swipeable cheatsheets (e.g. The 46-Week Pricing Formula, Payday Money Split) with clear "Save this for your next invoice" prompts.

02. Double Down on Carousels

11.3% Engagement Engine

Carousels deliver twice the engagement of reels. We will keep carousels as the core vehicle for client case studies and holistic finance breakdowns, rotating between editorial and clean studio layouts.

03. Reels for Top-of-Funnel

27 of 47 New Follows

Reels are the primary channel bringing new eyes to @thefinancewoman.co.uk. We will maintain high-quality aesthetic video clips with short, punchy opening hooks under 3 seconds to maximize non-follower distribution.

04. Story & Milestone Blends

Personal Brand Connection

Romany's milestone posts consistently achieve over 15% engagement. We should periodically interweave behind-the-scenes founder moments and industry recognitions alongside educational advice.
