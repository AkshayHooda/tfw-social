5. Key findings

  • Reach per post fell 34.8% (832 to 542). The previous period was lifted by award and personal milestone posts (Rising Star 2025, 30 Under 30), which reached around 1,000 to 1,200 each. This period was entirely educational.
  • Saves rose from 0.67 to 5.53 per 1,000 reach. 24 saves this month against 14 in the previous 11 months combined. Practical, reference-style carousels are building lasting value.
  • Four of six reels scored 5.5% engagement or less. Reels without Romany speaking, or without a direct question, are the weakest content on the account.
  • One reel produced 67% of the month's follows. "Busy business" (Romany on camera, a personal question) brought 6 of 9 follows and 17 of 30 comments.
  • Comments are concentrated. 2 posts produced 24 of 30 comments (80%). Most posts ask no question.

7. How the numbers are calculated

Measure Formula
Interactions Likes + Comments + Shares + Saves
Engagement rate Interactions / Reach * 100
Average reach per post Total reach / Number of posts
Saves per 1,000 reach Saves / Reach * 1,000
Shares per 1,000 reach Shares / Reach * 1,000
Follows per 1,000 reach Follows / Reach * 1,000
Views per account reached Views / Reach
Percentage change (This period - Previous period) / Previous period * 100
Example: engagement rate this period = 303 / 4,338 * 100 = 6.98%.

8. Notes and limits

  • Small sample: 8 posts in the period. Treat the findings as direction, not proof.
  • Reach is per post: total reach adds each post's reach, so people who saw several posts are counted more than once. It is not unique accounts reached.
  • Not included in this export: Stories, profile visits, link clicks, follower count, audience demographics and Facebook data.
  • Budget posts: posts published after 26 October, including the Budget 2026 carousels, are not in this export and will appear in next month's report.
  • Time zone: Meta export timestamps may be in Pacific time rather than UK time, so posting times should be checked against the scheduler before drawing timing conclusions.
