3. The 3 Creative Rules That Consistently Win

Across competitor audits and Romany's top-performing posts, three elements dictate whether a post converts or falls flat:

1. First 3 Seconds Hook: Video must begin with an uncomfortable or relatable truth ("An empty day in your diary isn't free", "Earned £99k on paper? Here is what HMRC does"). Never start with "Hi guys, today I want to talk about...".

2. Clear Math & Visual Breakdowns: Viewers stop scrolling when real numbers are shown on screen (e.g. 184 working days = day rate; £3,200 vet bill; £100k tax trap).

3. Single Direct Call-to-Action: Rather than asking people to like, comment, share, and book, every post focuses on one next step (e.g., "Book a free call: tidycal.com/romany" or "Comment LOST for the pension link").
